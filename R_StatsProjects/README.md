# 📊 R Statistical Modeling Projects (STAT 2602)

This folder highlights my R work from **STAT 2602: Statistical Models for Business Analytics & Finance**.  
It shows how I use R for analysis, visualization, model building, and interpretation.

---

## 📁 Projects
- **Assignment 1 (ANOVA & EDA)** — exploratory plots with `ggplot2` on `diamonds` and `mpg`; one-way ANOVA on rental length by car type; two-way ANOVA with a block (cloth tensile strength by chemical and bolt); and a **3×3 factorial design** on temperature × pressure and yield. *(See A1.pdf)*  
- **Assignment 2 (Regression & Model Selection)** — linear models with and without interaction on Insurance Innovation data; quadratic regression on a basketball drop to predict time to ground; **backward elimination** and **stepwise regression** to model CPI using Food, Shelter, Apparel, and Fuel Oil. *(See A2.pdf)*  
- **Assignment 3 (Logistic & Poisson)** — logistic regression for moth predation with distance, morph, and their interaction; deviance and Wald tests; goodness-of-fit and mild overdispersion check; Poisson modeling for elephant mating counts. *(See A3.pdf)*  
- **Assignment 4 (Time Series)** — exponential white noise; AR(1) simulation and forecasting; checking non-stationary vs stationary behavior; AR fit with confidence intervals; residual ACF diagnostics; trend modeling. *(See A4.pdf)*
