# 📊 R Statistical Modeling Projects (STAT 2602)

This folder highlights my R work from **STAT 2602: Statistical Models for Business Analytics & Finance**.  
Each assignment applies R for data analysis, visualization, and modeling.

---

## 📂 Assignments

- [Assignment 1 – ANOVA & EDA](AishaAbdisamad(101297747)A1.pdf)  
  Exploratory plots with `ggplot2` (diamonds, mpg), one-way ANOVA on rental length, two-way ANOVA with a block, and a **3×3 factorial design** on temperature × pressure and yield.

- [Assignment 2 – Regression & Model Selection](AishaAbdisamad(101297747)A2.pdf)  
  Linear models with and without interaction, quadratic regression on basketball drop data, and CPI models using **backward elimination** and **stepwise regression**.

- [Assignment 3 – Logistic & Poisson](AishaAbdisamad(101297747)A3.pdf)  
  Logistic regression for moth predation (distance, morph, interaction), deviance/Wald tests, goodness-of-fit, and Poisson regression for elephant matings.

- [Assignment 4 – Time Series](AishaAbdisamad(101297747)A4.pdf)  
  Simulations of exponential white noise and AR(1) processes, testing stationarity, ARIMA forecasting, and residual diagnostics.
